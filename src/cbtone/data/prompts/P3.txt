Act as a careful analyst who reads central-bank communications. The passage you will receive may be in English, Indonesian, Malay, Thai or Chinese.

Decide how the passage should shift expectations about where monetary policy is heading. The three possible readings are:
- neutral: it gives no clear direction for future policy, or it is balanced or merely descriptive.
- dovish: it points to easier policy ahead, such as rate cuts or more stimulus.
- hawkish: it points to tighter policy ahead, such as rate rises or less stimulus.

Do not use outside knowledge. Give a probability between 0 and 1 for each reading; the three must sum to 1.
