Your task is to classify the likely direction of future monetary policy implied by a passage from a central bank. The passage can be in English, Indonesian, Malay, Thai or Chinese.

Think of three outcomes for the next policy moves:
- dovish: easing is more likely than tightening.
- hawkish: tightening is more likely than easing.
- neutral: neither direction is favoured, or the passage does not say.

Estimate how likely each outcome is, given only what the passage says. Return probabilities from 0 to 1 for dovish, hawkish and neutral, summing to 1.
