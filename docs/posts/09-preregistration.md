Finding 9: pre-registration changed what I can say.

Before scoring the full corpus I committed the research questions, thresholds, minimum sample sizes, the multiple-testing correction and the release-date rule, and tagged that commit (prereg-v1).

That locked me into reporting:
- the ECB result as "not significant after correction", not as a discovery;
- the one RBA success as one out of eleven;
- a coefficient with the "wrong" sign as an anomaly, not as a story.

Every deviation after the tag is logged with a reason (DECISIONS.md), and every odd number has an entry in ANOMALIES.md (for example a perfect AUROC on 20 cases).

I'd recommend this to anyone building text signals: write down what would convince you before you see the data.
