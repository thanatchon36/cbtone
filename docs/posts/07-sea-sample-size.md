Finding 7: for Southeast Asian central banks, the binding constraint is data, not models.

- Bank Indonesia's statement archive starts in 2016.
- Bank Negara Malaysia changes its rate rarely, so there are few hikes and cuts to predict.
- MAS met twice a year until 2024 and sets an exchange-rate band, not an interest rate.

I set minimum sample sizes before looking at results. BNM and MAS fell below them for decision prediction, so they are reported, not tested.

Underpowered isn't the same as "no effect". It means these questions need either more years or pooled designs.

All sources are public, and the fetchers respect robots.txt and rate limits. For MAS and the PBoC the corpus uses Internet Archive copies, because the banks' sites block automated clients.
