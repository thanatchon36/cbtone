Finding 3: the same statement, two languages, two different scores.

Bank Indonesia and Bank Negara Malaysia publish their decisions in Indonesian/Malay and in English. I scored both versions of each statement with the same model and prompt.

They agree on direction: the stance flips (hawkish in one, dovish in the other) in only about 2% of pairs.

They disagree on strength: rank correlation 0.61 for BI and 0.53 for BNM, below the 0.70 bar I set in advance. The average gap is 0.28 on a -1 to +1 scale for BI.

If you build a tone index for a non-English-speaking central bank, the language you pick is a modelling choice and should be reported.

As far as I could find, this comparison has not been published before for central-bank text.
